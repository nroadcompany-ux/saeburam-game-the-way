# AWAKENING ENGINE

**Purpose:** Track and manifest the player's spiritual growth across 7 stages  
**Visibility:** Player sees their awakening state in UI + dialogue + NPC treatment  
**Binding:** All systems respond to awakening stage

---

## THE 7 STAGES

```
1. ASLEEP (잠든 자)
   ↓ Realization
2. CRACKED (균열)
   ↓ Call
3. CALLED (부르심)
   ↓ Encounter
4. CONFRONTED (대면)
   ↓ Turning
5. REPENTING (회개)
   ↓ Submission
6. OBEYING (순종)
   ↓ Endurance
7. FAITHFUL (충성)
```

---

## STAGE 1: ASLEEP (잠든 자)

**Duration:** Chapter 0 + Early Chapter 1

**Player State:**
- Unaware of true spiritual condition
- Living by own appetites
- Confident in self-sufficiency
- No awareness of God's presence

**In-Game Manifestation:**
- Player makes choices based on self-interest
- Dialogue reflects self-centeredness
- NPCs seem distant or mechanical
- Prayer (if any) feels empty

**Mechanics:**
- Faith meter starts at 30 (below center)
- Relationship options limited (NPCs don't open up)
- Dialogue reflects detachment
- Codex locked

**How Awakening Happens:**
- Something breaks the false comfort
- Reality intrudes (danger, loss, confrontation)
- First question asked: "What do you really want?"
- **Transition moment:** Player realizes something is wrong

**Exit condition:** First real doubt appears

**Codex entry:** "Awakening from Sleep"

---

## STAGE 2: CRACKED (균열)

**Duration:** Chapter 1, Early Acts 1-2

**Player State:**
- Sees the lie, but not yet the truth
- Uncomfortable with old worldview
- Sensing something bigger exists
- Resistant to change

**In-Game Manifestation:**
- Dialogue shows internal conflict
- Player notices NPCs treating them differently
- Questions start appearing in dialogue options
- Doubt becomes visible

**Mechanics:**
- Faith meter can fluctuate (30-50 range)
- Dialogue options change (can now question David)
- NPCs are curious about you ("You seem troubled")
- Codex begins unlocking (if you pay attention)

**Psychology:**
- Player feels discomfort (game purpose)
- Cognitive dissonance between old beliefs and new reality
- Temptation to return to sleep ("It was easier before")
- But cannot fully return (you've seen too much)

**Exit condition:** Player actively seeks answers

**Codex entry:** "The Cracks in My Certainty"

---

## STAGE 3: CALLED (부르심)

**Duration:** Chapter 1, Acts 2-3

**Player State:**
- Hearing God's voice (through circumstance, Scripture, people)
- Desire for something real is growing
- Still resisting, but less strongly
- Questions becoming more serious

**In-Game Manifestation:**
- David specifically addresses you with invitation
- Jonadab offers spiritual conversation
- Saul's presence becomes a challenge (can you resist him?)
- Prayer options become available

**Mechanics:**
- Faith meter can now rise significantly (50-70 available)
- Dialogue branches show calling ("Are you willing to learn?")
- NPCs invite you into their confidence
- Codex unlocks biblical context (if you choose to read)

**Emotional Tone:**
- Hope mixed with fear
- Attraction mixed with resistance
- Sense of "this matters"
- But not yet willing to commit

**Key Question:** "Will you hear the calling?"

**Exit condition:** Choice to listen or reject the call

**Codex entry:** "The Call"

---

## STAGE 4: CONFRONTED (대면)

**Duration:** Chapter 1, Acts 3-4 (The Cave Choice)

**Player State:**
- Direct encounter with God's reality
- No longer abstract
- No longer deniable
- Must respond NOW

**In-Game Manifestation:**
- The cave moment (Saul vulnerable)
- David's silence (waiting for you to choose)
- Abishai's pressure (choose violence)
- Jonadab's question (choose faith)
- **No escaping. Must choose.**

**Mechanics:**
- Faith meter now determines ending path
- This single choice reshapes everything after
- All dialogue becomes conditional on this choice
- No ambiguity in NPC response (they see who you really are)

**Emotional Tone:**
- Pressure
- Clarity
- Finality
- Trembling

**What This Stage Reveals:**
- Player's true values become visible
- Character is exposed
- Relationship with God becomes real or impossible
- No middle ground

**Exit condition:** The cave choice is made

**Codex entry:** "The Moment I Met God"

---

## STAGE 5: REPENTING (회개)

**Duration:** Chapter 1, Acts 4-5

**Player State:**
- Recognition of what was wrong
- Turning away from old path
- Moving toward new direction
- Still afraid, but committed

**In-Game Manifestation:**
- Dialogue reflects change (or, if you chose badly, regret)
- David's response shows acceptance or disappointment
- Relationships shift fundamentally
- Past choices now have weight (cannot be undone)

**Mechanics:**
- Faith meter stabilizes at new level
- If you chose mercy: 70+ (Believer path opening)
- If you chose killing: 20-40 (Burden path opening)
- If you chose betrayal: 0-20 (Betrayer path continuing)
- NPC relationships set (cannot be easily repaired)

**Repentance Manifestation:**
- "I can't undo what I did, but I can move forward"
- Confession becomes possible (dialogue with David/Jonadab)
- Acceptance of consequences (not running from them)
- Commitment to new way (even if cost is high)

**Key Truth:** Repentance includes accepting consequence

**Exit condition:** Player walks forward into new life

**Codex entry:** "The Turning"

---

## STAGE 6: OBEYING (순종)

**Duration:** Chapter 1, Acts 5 + Post-game

**Player State:**
- Following the Way, not by emotion, but by choice
- Understanding the cost (but not stopping)
- Walking through desert (metaphorical and literal)
- Trusting what cannot be seen

**In-Game Manifestation:**
- Dialogue shows alignment with God's direction
- NPCs respect your steady commitment
- Choices are harder (not easier), but clearer
- Prayer feels active (not empty)

**Mechanics:**
- Faith meter maintained at high level (60-80+)
- Obedience choices available (dangerous, hard, counterintuitive)
- Each obedience increases relationship with David
- Codex fully open (all wisdom available)

**Spiritual Challenge:**
- Emotion has faded but commitment remains
- Suffering is present (faith doesn't remove suffering)
- Temptation to return to old life is gone (can't go back)
- But temptation to take shortcuts is strong

**Example Scenarios:**
- "I'm hungry. Should I take this extra ration from the group?"
- "David made a mistake. Should I follow anyway?"
- "I could escape. Should I stay?"

**Key Truth:** Obedience is a practice, not a moment

**Exit condition:** Player demonstrates consistent obedience despite cost

**Codex entry:** "The Way of the Obedient"

---

## STAGE 7: FAITHFUL (충성)

**Duration:** Post-Chapter 1 / Implied Future

**Player State:**
- Faithfulness is now character, not effort
- Following through regardless of feeling
- Transformed from inside out
- Ready to lead others

**In-Game Manifestation:**
- This stage is mostly revealed in endings
- But if reached: Player sees themselves as David sees them
- New mission options available (lead, teach, go deeper)
- Relationship with God is mature (not perfect, but steady)

**Mechanics:**
- Faith meter at 75-100 (if sustained)
- All dialogue with David becomes peer-level
- New quests available (serving others)
- Codex shows life pattern (not just information)

**What This Stage Is NOT:**
- Not perfection (still struggles)
- Not always happy (suffering continues)
- Not guaranteed (must be renewed daily)
- Not ending (this is ongoing)

**What This Stage IS:**
- Identity transformation (you ARE faithful, not just trying)
- Loyalty proven through test
- Readiness to help others on their journey
- Deep peace alongside ongoing struggle

**Key Truth:** Faithfulness is the only guaranteed ending for those who stay

**Exit condition:** This is the goal (not reached in one game, but started)

**Codex entry:** "The Long Faithfulness"

## MECHANICAL IMPLEMENTATION

### Awakening State Visibility

**Player Dashboard Shows:**
```
Awakening Stage: [Visual progress bar]
Current Stage: OBEYING (6/7)
Time at this stage: 2 hours 45 minutes

Next milestone:
→ Faithful Demonstration (unknown requirement)
```

**NPC Recognition:**
- David: "You've become like a real believer"
- Jonadab: "Your faith is solid now"
- Abishai: "You don't flinch like you used to"

### Dialogue Reflects Stage

**Same situation, different dialogue per stage:**

**Stage 2 (Cracked):**
- David: "Will you learn from this?"
- Option: "I don't know what you're asking"

**Stage 5 (Repenting):**
- David: "Will you turn from this?"
- Option: "I see my error. Help me change"

**Stage 7 (Faithful):**
- David: "Will you lead others this way?"
- Option: "Yes. I know what this costs, and I'll show them"

### Faith Meter Interaction

**Faith meter and Awakening are linked but separate:**

- Faith (0-100): Measure of trust in God's way
- Awakening (1-7): Stage of transformation

A player can have:
- High faith (75), Stage 5 (Repenting) = Believer who just converted
- Low faith (35), Stage 6 (Obeying) = Faithfully following despite doubt
- Medium faith (55), Stage 3 (Called) = Hearing the call but not yet committed

## STAGE TRANSITIONS

### From Stage 1 → 2: The Crack

**Triggered by:**
- Moment of cognitive dissonance (what you believed ≠ what's real)
- Personal crisis (loss, danger, failure)
- Encounter with faith (seeing David pray, seeing consequences of faith)
- Question that won't go away ("Is there more than this?")

**Player feels:** Unease, curiosity, resistance

### From Stage 2 → 3: The Call

**Triggered by:**
- Explicit invitation (David asks directly)
- Recurring question (multiple NPCs asking same thing)
- Moment of beauty (sunrise, prayer, grace)
- Realization (this isn't coincidence, this is invitation)

**Player feels:** Drawn, but hesitant

### From Stage 3 → 4: The Confrontation

**Triggered by:**
- The cave choice (unavoidable decision)
- Moment of truth (can no longer sit on fence)
- High stakes (choice has eternal weight)
- Clarity (path is clear, even if hard)

**Player feels:** Pressure, clarity, fear, possibility

### From Stage 4 → 5: The Repentance

**Triggered by:**
- Seeing consequence of choice
- Realizing you can't undo it
- Understanding new direction is possible
- David's response (acceptance or challenge)

**Player feels:** Grief, relief, commitment, awe

### From Stage 5 → 6: The Obedience

**Triggered by:**
- Consistent daily choices aligned with God
- Realization that emotion has faded but commitment remains
- Test faced and passed
- New confidence in following

**Player feels:** Steadiness, peace despite struggle, clarity

### From Stage 6 → 7: The Faithfulness

**Triggered by:**
- Long period of consistent obedience
- Major test endured without wavering
- Willingness to help others on the path
- Recognition that transformation is complete (but ongoing)

**Player feels:** Peace, purpose, readiness for mission

---

## SPECIAL: "The Backslide"

A player can regress.

**Example:**
- Stage 5 (Repenting) → Makes selfish choice → Stage 4 (Confronted) again
- But now with new understanding
- Higher chance of passing the test the second time

**Mechanics:**
- Going backwards is possible (game allows for failure)
- But each time, the cost is higher
- Grace is always available (no "locked" endings until final choice)
- Redemption is always possible (even betrayers have an open door)

---

## THE ARC ACROSS GAME

**Chapter 0:** Stage 1 → 2 (Asleep → Cracked)

**Chapter 1, Acts 1-2:** Stage 2 → 3 (Cracked → Called)

**Chapter 1, Act 3:** Stage 3 → 4 (Called → Confronted)

**Chapter 1, Acts 4-5:** Stage 4 → 5 → 6 (Confronted → Repenting → Obeying)

**Post-Game (Implied):** Stage 6 → 7 (Obeying → Faithful)

---

## THE PLAYER'S QUESTION AT EACH STAGE

**Stage 1 (Asleep):** "Is there more than this?"
**Stage 2 (Cracked):** "What if I'm wrong?"
**Stage 3 (Called):** "Should I listen?"
**Stage 4 (Confronted):** "Will I choose God or myself?"
**Stage 5 (Repenting):** "Can I really change?"
**Stage 6 (Obeying):** "Can I keep choosing this way?"
**Stage 7 (Faithful):** "How do I help others find this way?"

## FINAL NOTES

The AWAKENING ENGINE is not imposed on the player.

It unfolds naturally through the story.

Most players won't consciously notice the stages.

They'll just **feel themselves changing.**

And at the end, they'll realize:

**"I'm not the same person who started."**

That is the point.

That is the entire purpose.

**THE WAY transforms.**
