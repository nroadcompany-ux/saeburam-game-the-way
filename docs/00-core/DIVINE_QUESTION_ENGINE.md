# DIVINE QUESTION ENGINE

**Purpose:** Every quest is built on a divine question, not a task  
**Output:** Meaningful choice, consequential result, spiritual growth  
**Binding:** Non-negotiable quest structure

---

## THE ENGINE: Question → Choice → Result → Revelation

```
┌─ DIVINE QUESTION ─────────────────────────────┐
│  "What do you truly love?"                     │
│  "Can you trust me?"                          │
│  "Will you follow?"                          │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                    ↓
┌─ CHOICE PRESENTED ────────────────────────────┐
│  3-5 options, no "right" answer               │
│  Each reveals character                        │
│  All have weight                               │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                    ↓
┌─ RESULT UNFOLDS ──────────────────────────────┐
│  Immediate: Relationship changes              │
│  NPC reactions are real                        │
│  Consequences are irreversible                │
│  Faith meter reflects direction               │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────┘
                    ↓
┌─ REVELATION STRIKES ──────────────────────────┐
│  Why this choice mattered spiritually         │
│  What it reveals about character              │
│  Scripture illuminates (not explains)         │
│  Growth becomes visible                        │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## THE 12 DIVINE QUESTIONS

These 12 questions are the **core curriculum** of the game.

Every quest, every choice, every consequence points back to one of these.

### Question 1: "너는 무엇을 원하느냐" (What do you truly want?)

**Divine purpose:** Expose the idol in your heart

**In-game moment:** 
- Player faces a choice where what they "think" they want ≠ what they "really" want
- Example: "I want safety" vs "I want justice" vs "I want peace"

**Mechanical effect:**
- Choice reveals priority
- Faith meter reflects real desire
- NPC sees who you truly are

**Codex unlock:** "The Desires of the Heart"

---

### Question 2: "네가 원하는 것이 정말 나냐" (Is what you want really you?)

**Divine purpose:** Distinguish between self-desire and self-deception

**In-game moment:**
- Player wanted something, got it, discovered it's hollow
- Or: Player thought they wanted revenge, but peace is better
- Or: Material success turned to ashes

**Mechanical effect:**
- Dialogue option becomes unavailable (character doesn't want it anymore)
- Faith meter rises if you recognize the lie
- Relationship deepens with those who understand

**Codex unlock:** "The Illusion of Wanting"

---

### Question 3: "너는 나를 사랑하느냐" (Do you love me?)

**Divine purpose:** Test the basis of faith

**In-game moment:**
- Simple question, but asked at cost
- Saying yes means losing something
- David asks during hardship

**Mechanical effect:**
- This choice locks your path
- All subsequent choices flow from this answer
- No going back

**Codex unlock:** "The Cost of Love"

---

### Question 4: "너는 진정으로 나를 사랑하느냐" (Do you TRULY love me?)

**Divine purpose:** Test the depth of faith

**In-game moment:**
- Asked after player has been tested
- After suffering, doubt, cost
- More demanding than question 3

**Mechanical effect:**
- Even higher consequence
- Determines ending path
- Changes final dialogue available

**Codex unlock:** "The Depth of Devotion"

---

### Question 5: "너는 무엇 때문에 나를 찾고 있느냐" (Why are you seeking me?)

**Divine purpose:** Expose whether faith is for blessing or relationship

**In-game moment:**
- Player realizes they sought God for wrong reasons
- Example: For power, not relationship
- Example: For escape, not transformation

**Mechanical effect:**
- Honest answer unlocks redemption path
- False answer locks you deeper
- NPC (David/Jonadab) sees through facade

**Codex unlock:** "Seeking With Wrong Motives"

---

### Question 6: "너는 무엇을 버릴 수 있느냐" (What can you surrender?)

**Divine purpose:** Test the seriousness of commitment

**In-game moment:**
- Player must choose to lose something real
- Not hypothetical. Not future. **Now.**
- Examples:
  - Opportunity for revenge
  - Chance for wealth
  - Safety of the group
  - Your own reputation

**Mechanical effect:**
- Permanent loss (not recoverable)
- Faith meter rises significantly
- Dialogue with David deepens

**Codex unlock:** "The Sacrifice"

---

### Question 7: "너는 정말 순종할 수 있느냐" (Can you truly obey?)

**Divine purpose:** Distinguish between intellectual assent and volitional obedience

**In-game moment:**
- Command that doesn't make sense
- Instruction that contradicts reason
- Example: Don't kill the enemy when you have him cornered

**Mechanical effect:**
- Obedience WITHOUT understanding (+8 faith)
- Obedience WITH resistance (+5 faith)
- Disobedience WITH guilt (-3 faith)
- Disobedience WITHOUT guilt (-10 faith)

**Codex unlock:** "Obedience Without Understanding"

---

### Question 8: "너는 정말 충성할 수 있느냐" (Can you truly remain faithful?)

**Divine purpose:** Test the permanence of commitment

**In-game moment:**
- Temptation to abandon the path
- Suffering that tests loyalty
- Moment where returning to old life is possible
- **But player chooses to stay**

**Mechanical effect:**
- Choosing to stay despite cost: +12 faith (highest gain)
- All relationships deepen
- Unlocks final/secret dialogue

**Codex unlock:** "The Long Faithfulness"

---

### Question 9: "네가 구하는 것을 알고 구하느냐" (Do you seek with full knowledge?)

**Divine purpose:** Ensure informed consent to the spiritual path

**In-game moment:**
- At the point of irreversible decision
- Player must understand what they're choosing
- Example: Mercy over justice (this costs forever)

**Mechanical effect:**
- Choosing WITH understanding of cost: highest credibility
- Choosing WITHOUT understanding: still valid but unstable
- Dialogue reflects this understanding level

**Codex unlock:** "The Informed Choice"

---

### Question 10: "네가 누구를 부르는지 알고 부르느냐" (Do you call on one you truly know?)

**Divine purpose:** Distinguish between idol-worship and God-worship

**In-game moment:**
- Prayer moment where character's real faith is tested
- Is it habitual? Desperate? Genuine?
- Does the prayer reveal knowledge of God's character?

**Mechanical effect:**
- Authentic prayer: Dialogue shifts, David respects you
- Desperate prayer: Grace extended, but confusion remains
- Habitual prayer: God seems distant, doubts increase

**Codex unlock:** "Knowing God's Name"

---

### Question 11: "세상의 일이 나를 위한 것이냐, 너를 위한 것이냐" (Are things for my glory or for God's?)

**Divine purpose:** Shift from self-centered to God-centered worldview

**In-game moment:**
- Major event happens
- Player must interpret its meaning
- Is God's glory shown or mine?

**Mechanical effect:**
- Interpreting events as "God's work" despite cost: +7 faith
- Interpreting as "my luck": 0 faith change
- Interpreting as "God's judgment on me": -5 faith

**Codex unlock:** "The Divine Narrative"

---

### Question 12: "너는 나를 따르겠느냐" (Will you follow me?)

**Divine purpose:** Final commitment to the Way

**In-game moment:**
- Asked at the climactic decision point
- The cave moment (kill/spare Saul)
- Everything culminates here

**Mechanical effect:**
- Yes (with full faith): Believer's ending
- Yes (with doubt): Seeker's ending
- No (choosing self): Wanderer's ending
- Betrayal (choosing enemy): Betrayer's ending

**Codex unlock:** "The Final Question"

## HOW THIS ENGINE WORKS IN PRACTICE

### Quest Structure

Every major quest follows this pattern:

**Act 1: SITUATION**
- Present moral/spiritual tension
- Introduce the divine question (subtly)
- Player doesn't yet know it's being asked

**Act 2: CHOICE**
- The divine question becomes explicit (or implicit through options)
- 3-5 paths available
- All are defensible

**Act 3: RESULT**
- Consequence unfolds
- NPC reacts
- Player sees impact of choice

**Act 4: REVELATION**
- Divine truth connected to the question
- Scripture illuminates (not preaches)
- Codex entry unlocks
- Growth becomes visible to player

### Example: "The Cave Choice" (Quest 3)

**Divine Question Being Asked:** "Will you follow me?" (Q12) + "Can you trust me?" (implied)

**Act 1: SITUATION**
- Saul enters cave, defenseless
- Abishai urges: Kill him now
- Jonadab questions: Is this God's will?
- David is silent (waiting for you)
- Player doesn't consciously realize: "Will I follow God's way or my own?"

**Act 2: CHOICE**
```
Option A: Kill Saul (pragmatic)
→ Reveals: You trust your sword more than God's justice

Option B: Spare him (merciful)
→ Reveals: You trust God's judgment more than immediate safety

Option C: Alert David (deferential)
→ Reveals: You defer authority but keep moral distance

Option D: Betray group (selfish)
→ Reveals: You serve yourself above all
```

**Act 3: RESULT**
- Kill path: Victory feels hollow. David is heartbroken. Group divides.
- Spare path: Saul leaves humbled. David recognizes your faith. Deepest bond forms.
- Alert path: You did the right thing, but someone else got credit.
- Betray path: Saul uses you. David hunts forever. You become agent of persecution.

**Act 4: REVELATION**
- All paths: Codex entry "The Final Question"
- Scripture: "Vengeance is mine, says the Lord"
- Truth: "Whether you trust me determines everything that comes next"

### Question Tracker

Each question answered honestly raises faith.

Evasion or dishonesty lowers faith.

The pattern shows character arc:
- Questions 1-4: Entering the Way
- Questions 5-8: Walking the Way
- Questions 9-12: Committing to the Way

## WRITING GUIDELINES FOR DIVINE QUESTIONS

### The Question Must Be Asked Through STORY, Not Exposition

**NOT:**
```
David: "This situation asks: Can you trust me?"
```

**YES:**
```
David: "Saul is asleep. We could end this."
(Silence)
David: "What will you do?"
```

The question is **implied** in the situation.

### The Question Must Create Real Tension

**NOT:**
```
Jonadab: "We should pray about whether to kill him."
```

**YES:**
```
Abishai: "Now. Before his eyes open."
Jonadab: "David should decide."
(You sense: This moment will define everything)
```

### The Answer Must Show CHARACTER

**NOT:**
```
(Player chooses "Kill Saul")
(Game says: "You chose violence!")
```

**YES:**
```
(Player chooses "Kill Saul")
(Abishai nods: "I knew you were a warrior")
(David looks away: He doesn't speak)
(Jonadab leaves: No words needed)
```

## THE AWAKENING ARC

The 12 questions follow the player's spiritual awakening:

```
Questions 1-2 (Awakening from Self-Deception)
→ Questions 3-4 (Initial Commitment)
→ Questions 5-6 (Cost Acceptance)
→ Questions 7-8 (Obedience in Practice)
→ Questions 9-10 (Knowledge & Prayer)
→ Questions 11-12 (Final Transformation)
```

By the end, the player has answered:

**"Who am I?" → "Who is God?" → "Will I follow?"**
